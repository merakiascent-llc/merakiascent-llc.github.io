Common Questions

Before you reach out.

Who does brand photography and video in the Methow Valley?

Meraki Ascent LLC, founded by Hannah Weymuller, provides brand photography, videography, and content creation for businesses in the Methow Valley, including Winthrop, Twisp, Mazama, and Carlton, and across Okanogan County and Chelan, Washington.

How much does a photo or video shoot cost?

Shoots are quoted per project. Half-day and full-day on-site shoots can be booked on their own or added to any Meraki Ascent social media package. Book a free 30-minute discovery call to get a written quote.

Do you create UGC for outdoor brands?

Yes. Meraki Ascent creates UGC-style photo and video for outdoor, ski, bike, and lifestyle brands, shot in real terrain in the Methow Valley and across Washington. Usage rights and deliverables are agreed in writing before the shoot.

Do you shoot video as well as photos?

Yes. Meraki Ascent shoots and edits short-form vertical video, reels, short brand films, and event recaps, as well as still photography.

Where do you travel for shoots?

Meraki Ascent is based in Okanogan County, Washington, and regularly shoots in Winthrop, Twisp, Mazama, Carlton, Omak, Okanogan, Brewster, Pateros, and Chelan. Projects elsewhere in Washington are available by arrangement.
