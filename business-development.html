Common Questions

Before you reach out.

What is fractional business development?

Fractional business development means a senior business development lead working part-time for your company. Meraki Ascent builds partnership strategy, target lists, outreach, and pipeline systems for founder-led companies that are not ready for a full-time hire.

What does investor relations support include?

Meraki Ascent helps founders and emerging funds with investor updates and letters, pitch narrative, investor communications calendars, and relationship tracking. It is communications and relationship support. Meraki Ascent is not a broker-dealer and does not offer or sell securities.

Who does Meraki Ascent work with?

Values-driven founders building for the long term, and impact investors, family offices, and venture funds focused on sustainable returns.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Business development and investor relations work is done remotely for clients across Washington and the United States, from Okanogan County, Washington.

How is Meraki Ascent connected to Terra Axis?

Hannah Weymuller is Managing Director, Investor Relations and Business Development at Terra Axis. Meraki Ascent LLC and Terra Axis are separate companies.
