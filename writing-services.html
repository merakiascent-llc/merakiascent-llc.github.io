Common Questions

Do you ghostwrite LinkedIn posts for founders?

Yes. Meraki Ascent ghostwrites LinkedIn posts and articles for founders and executives, working from voice interviews so the writing sounds like the founder, not like a template.

What kinds of writing does Meraki Ascent do?

Founder LinkedIn ghostwriting, website and About page copy, articles and blog posts, investor updates and pitch narratives, brand voice guides, and newsletters.
