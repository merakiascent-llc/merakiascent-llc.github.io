Common Questions

Questions about Meraki Ascent

Who does social media management in the Methow Valley?

Meraki Ascent LLC is a marketing and social media management practice serving businesses in the Methow Valley, Winthrop, Twisp, Mazama, Carlton, and across Okanogan County, WA. Founded by Hannah Weymuller, Meraki Ascent offers full Instagram management, content strategy, and marketing consulting for founder-led, values-driven businesses.

Where can I find a marketing consultant near Winthrop or Twisp, WA?

Meraki Ascent LLC is a marketing consultant based in Okanogan County, Washington, serving Winthrop, Twisp, Mazama, Carlton and businesses across the Methow Valley and Okanogan County. Book a 30-minute discovery call at merakiascent.com/contact.

What social media services does Meraki Ascent offer?

Meraki Ascent offers three packaged social media tiers: Roots (entry), Foothills (growth), and Summit (executive). All packages include content strategy, community management, and posting. Beyond social media, Meraki Ascent provides business development, investor relations, and founder advisory consulting.

Who runs Meraki Ascent?

Meraki Ascent LLC is founded and run by Hannah Weymuller, a marketing consultant and social media manager based in Okanogan County, Washington, who works with businesses across the Methow Valley. Hannah has 5+ years of experience in revenue operations, business development, and marketing strategy across founder-led companies, impact funds, and nonprofits.

What businesses does Meraki Ascent work with?

Meraki Ascent's current client roster includes Methow Cycle & Sport (Winthrop, WA), East 20 Pizza (Winthrop, WA), the Town of Winthrop (Winthrop, WA), and Twisp River Suites (Twisp, WA). Past clients include Gamble Sands (Brewster, WA) and Blue Star Coffee Roasters (Twisp, WA). Meraki Ascent works with founder-led, values-driven businesses in Okanogan County and across Washington State.
