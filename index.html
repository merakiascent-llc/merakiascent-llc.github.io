The Founder

Strategic operator.
Human connector.

Born and raised in Winthrop. Outdoor enthusiast and athlete, the kind of person who finishes one goal and is already eyeing the next one. Managing Partner at Terra Axis with five-plus years building revenue operations, business development, and marketing strategy across founder-led companies, impact funds, nonprofits, and higher education programs. Most recently led social and digital for Gamble Sands, one of the most-watched golf destinations in the Pacific Northwest.

Alongside client work, I've founded and grown my own small-scale brands in the health and wellness and pet spaces, earning brand partnerships and proving the playbook from the inside out.

Meraki Ascent is the practice I built around three things I deeply believe in: passion for the work, purpose in every connection, and progress that's measurable and real.
